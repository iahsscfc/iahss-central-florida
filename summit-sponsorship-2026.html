Sponsorship Opportunities

Support healthcare security education and professional networking across Florida.

November 12, 2026 · Tampa, Florida

Become a Summit Sponsor

The Second Annual IAHSS Florida Joint Chapter Summit will be held at Embassy Suites by Hilton in downtown Tampa, adjacent to the Tampa Convention Center. Select the sponsorship level that best fits your organization.

Diamond Sponsorship

$6,000

Two complimentary registrations and two six-foot tables or equivalent vendor display space at a premier location. Includes keynote and/or guest speaker introduction, vendor logo recognition at the event and in digital event communications, plus the registered attendee email list. Attendee email lists are provided to Diamond and Platinum sponsors only.

Platinum Sponsorship

$3,000

One complimentary registration, opening and/or guest speaker introduction, and one six-foot table or equivalent vendor display space at a secondary location. Includes logo recognition at the event and in digital event communications, plus the registered attendee email list. Attendee email lists are provided to Diamond and Platinum sponsors only.

Gold Sponsorship

$1,500

Registrations are not included. Includes one six-foot table or equivalent vendor display space at a designated location and Gold sponsor logo recognition at the event and in digital event communications. The registered attendee email list is not provided at this level.

Silver Sponsorship

$750

Registrations are not included. Includes the ability to distribute promotional items and Silver sponsor logo recognition at the event and in digital event communications. The registered attendee email list is not provided at this level.
