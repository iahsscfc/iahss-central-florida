IAHSS comes to Tampa

Three days of education, networking and industry connection.

The IAHSS Annual Conference & Exhibition brings healthcare security, safety and emergency management professionals together for education, peer exchange and access to industry partners.

The 2027 conference will be held at the JW Marriott Tampa Water Street and is currently promoted by National IAHSS as both in-person and virtual.
