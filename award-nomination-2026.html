Award criteria

Choose the best category

Merit Award

Personal fortitude above and beyond the call of duty, without risking the nominee's own life.

Excellence Award

An outstanding healthcare security or safety program, regular act or duty.

Valor Award

A selfless or courageous act performed with full awareness of danger and risk to the nominee's own life.

Distinction Award

A significant and lasting contribution to IAHSS or the healthcare security profession.

Security Officer / Dispatcher of the Year

A lasting contribution, consistent performance above and beyond duty, and exemplary service to the nominee's agency.
