2026 Healthcare Security & Safety Week

Healthcare Security and Safety Week is an opportunity to recognize the professionals whose commitment, vigilance and service help create safer environments for patients, visitors, caregivers and staff.

The IAHSS Central Florida Chapter proudly thanks healthcare security and safety professionals throughout Central Florida and across the profession for the work they do every day in support of quality patient care.

October 14

Officer Appreciation Day

Wednesday, October 14 is Healthcare Security and Safety Officer Appreciation Day. We encourage healthcare leaders, colleagues and community partners to take a moment to recognize the officers serving their organizations.

Professional development

IAHSS HSSW Webinar

IAHSS will present Turning Data into Action: Strategies to Reduce Assault-Related Injuries in Healthcare on Wednesday, October 14 at noon Eastern. The session offers 1 CHPA/CE credit.
